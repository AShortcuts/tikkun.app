# Private correction surface

Mode: Operate. Existing Tikkun reader typography and calm authoring controls
are the visual authority. The user's delegated implementation approval governs
routine layout choices; this is a code-first private extension.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Listen, locate, correct. The Hebrew passage is the working surface.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper, dark ink, restrained blue selection, Tikkun's local
Lora and Stam fonts. Neutral tool surfaces and conventional controls.

STORY: Choose a recording, hear a word, inspect its waveform, adjust timing,
mark review, and retain a private draft.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact recording header above a large Hebrew passage and
right-side word inspector. A full-width waveform and transport anchor the
bottom. Mobile stacks passage, inspector, then transport without sideways scroll.

FORM: Direct extension of the established reader and authoring workflow;
no replacement visual world or concept seed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
