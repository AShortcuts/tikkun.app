## Audience and truth

keriah. Central job: move from supported reading and listening to independent
practice. The strongest proven benefit is a real tikkun with optional nekudot,
available recordings, and synchronized word highlighting where timing exists.
Emotional tone: focused, reassuring, respectful. No guaranteed mastery claims.

## Art direction

Considered: (A) blue architectural linework around devices, (B) warm editorial
study edition with Lora and ample margins, (C) all-dark audio-led posters.
Selected B: best fit for careful Hebrew reading; a few blue and charcoal sheets
give rhythm. Prior Tikkun delivery was unframed native images, not an approved
marketing composition. This run adds distinct storytelling and editorial layout.
It does not borrow Yahrtzeit's palette, headlines, or typography pair.

Lora Regular is already a Tikkun brand font. Small supporting copy uses Helvetica
Neue, with hierarchy led by Lora. Palette: paper #f4f0e7, ink #202b32, blue #146ac3,
pale blue #e7eff7, warm yellow #f5cf52, charcoal #20262d. Thin rules evoke a printed
study edition. No fabricated Hebrew text or decorative sacred passages.

Use authentic Apple silver iPhone 17 Pro Max and iPad Pro 13-inch bezels already
available locally. Preserve geometry and original native captures. Mix cropped
hero devices, complete devices, and a few enlarged native detail crops. Detail
crops are labeled as details and paired with the same native full-device view.
No fake UI, recoloring, or diagonal seams between different layouts.
