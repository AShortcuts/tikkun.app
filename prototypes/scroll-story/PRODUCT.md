# Tikkun Reader Scroll Story Prototype

Status: isolated design draft. It is not approved to replace the current public landing page.

## Product

Tikkun Reader is a Torah-reading companion by Ocean of Torah. Its unique mechanism keeps a recording, the active word, the aliyah boundary, and the original Torah layout connected during practice.

The primary audience is a Torah reader preparing a specific aliyah on a phone or computer. This surface must make the practice workflow clear, demonstrate the real reader, and provide a direct path into the selected reading.

## Approved Truth

- The current public landing page is the visual and structural baseline.
- The earlier redesign contributes only its "Read it your way" theme section.
- The Beresheet hero uses one functional same-origin reader without changing the reader implementation. Desktop presents it in Dark with a cool-white illuminated frame; mobile keeps it upright and themeable.
- The theme controls default to Light and affect only the visible prototype reader. On mobile they rise with an attached paper sheet; on desktop they remain in the dedicated paper chapter. They do not write reader preferences.
- Existing status colors, aliyah deep links, internal tools, and reader routes remain product truth.
- The practice story demonstrates three different real flows: opening contents to an aliyah deep link, listening with word context, and continuing from a recent place.
- The ambient hero asset stays confined to the hero and is preloaded.
- Mobile is the primary responsive constraint.

## Direction Record

- Mode: Persuade, supported by a real Operate/Read proof in the live reader.
- Chosen form: preserve-led scroll story.
- Selection: user-pinned direction, one of one. A concept roll was not used because the user specified the incumbent, references, retained section, brand, interaction model, and prototype boundary.
- FORM seed key: `PINNED-SCROLL-STORY-2026-08-09`.
- Quality bar: the current Tikkun landing page for identity, Sentient OS for scroll rhythm, and Apple MacBook Pro for a deliberate dark-to-light chapter transition.

## Boundaries

- All design work remains under the prototype route or prototype documentation folders until explicit approval.
- The current landing page, existing Apple/Sentient prototype, reader, taskboard, cue analytics, and internal authoring surfaces are outside this draft's change boundary.
- Theme controls change only the embedded reader in the section. They do not write reader preferences or change the whole page theme.
