# What the reader holds today

Inventory taken 2026-11-02 from the running source, as the starting point for the navigation redesign. "Overflow menu" is the ⋯ Reader controls menu in the reader header.

## Choosing a reading

| Feature | Where it lives now | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Library: Continue Reading, Coming Up, Torah books, parshiot, holidays, megillot | Reader header button (Readings on iPhone, Home on the web) opens the Library panel; desktop also shows a Reading Calendar | The main way in, but it is a panel over the reader, not a place of its own |
| Search readings, references and settings | Top of the Library; also Cmd/Ctrl+K command palette | Settings are searchable, but only from here |
| Bookmarks | Bookmark button in the header and overflow menu; saved bookmarks appear only as search results | No bookmarks list exists |
| Resume where you left off | "Resume reading?" bar under the toolbar, and Continue Reading in the Library | Two entry points for one thing |
| Choose an aliyah | Aliyah button in the header, the aliyah rail, the overflow menu and the floating player | Four entry points |
| Show aliyah starts | Overflow menu | |

## Settings (gear button, or overflow menu > Reader Settings)

Four icon-only tabs:

| Tab | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Reading | Layout: line layout, what to show, font size, pasuk numbers, sides. Reader: reading center, Focal Measure, Disable Shift for Nekudot |
| Appearance | Theme and custom themes; Current Word highlight (fill, border, spacing, corners, halo) |
| Playback | Reduced motion; Ba'al Koreh; playback speed; auto-scroll with playback |
| More | Print passage; Offline (Torah pages, recordings, a Media & Storage button); App Updates (iPhone); Privacy; Support and diagnostic report |

## Hidden or operator-only

Cue authoring (unlocked admin panel), recording mode, cue analytics, focal debug measure, the reveal gesture, update and App Store prompt previews.

## What this shows

- Settings > More is a catch-all: printing, downloads, updates, privacy and support share one icon-only tab.
- Media & Storage, printing and saving audio are real features with no place of their own.
- Bookmarks can be saved but not browsed.
- Some actions have up to four entry points (choosing an aliyah), while others have one hidden one (saving audio).
- The Library is the app's home, but it opens as a panel on top of the reader.
